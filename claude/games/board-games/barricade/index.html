Rules
Complete guide to STEENE
01
Objective
Before the game starts, each player secretly selects target squares on their opponent's starting row (2 by default — the exact number matches your chosen Barricade Mode). To win, all of your pieces must land on your secret targets at the same time. Targets stay hidden until the game ends.
02
Setup
The board size, piece count, and barricade stock are all configurable before a match:

• Board Size — 8×8, 10×10, or 12×12
• Pieces per Player — Single, Double (default), Triple, or Quadruple
• Barricades — split evenly between horizontal and vertical, scaled to board size

Players choose secret targets privately before play begins. White moves first.

Configurable board & pieces Barricades scale with board size
03
Movement — 4 Directions Only
On your turn, you either move a piece or place a barricade — never both. A move is exactly 1 square Up, Down, Left, or Right. No diagonals.

04
Jumping Over an Opponent
If an opponent's piece is directly in front of you (the square you want to move into), you jump straight over them and land on the square beyond — as long as that landing square is empty and not blocked by a wall.

You never land on the opponent or push them. If the square beyond them is occupied or walled off, you simply cannot move in that direction.
●
White jumps straight over Blue and lands 2 squares ahead — orange shows the path, gold dot shows the landing square.

Tactic: Approach opponents head-on to leap past them
05
Barricades — Block the Opponent's Path
Instead of moving, you may place one barricade on any open grid line between two cells — horizontal or vertical — from your remaining stock.

A barricade blocks movement straight through that line — pieces cannot cross it, and it also blocks jumps that would otherwise pass over an opponent through that line.

Important restriction: you can never place a barricade that would completely seal off any piece with no possible route remaining to its owner's target squares. The game checks this automatically and prevents illegal placements.
A horizontal wall placed below White blocks movement/jumps straight through that edge.

Use your barricades to carve a maze that favors your route
06
Victory
You win when all your pieces simultaneously occupy your secret target squares. Victory is checked after every move. Targets are revealed to both players on the victory screen.

Your opponent is watching your moves and walls, trying to deduce your targets to block you. Misdirection and clever wall placement are everything.
