STEENE CHECKERS ACADEMY

Learn the board like a competitor.

Understand the rhythm first. Then learn how to break it.

01

Movement

Men move diagonally forward by one dark square. Kings move diagonally in both directions.

02

Open Capture

If a capture exists, you may still choose a quiet legal move. That is this arena's signature house rule.

03

Jumping

Jump over an adjacent enemy into the empty square behind it. The jumped piece leaves the board.

04

Multi-jumps

Once you begin a jump sequence, the same piece continues while another capture remains available.

05

Crowning

Reach the far rank and your man becomes a king. Kings are the tactical swing pieces of the endgame.

06

Winning

Win by removing every opposing piece or leaving the opponent without a legal move.

✦
Study tip

Against the higher AI tiers, don't just ask “What can I take?” Ask “What happens after I take it?”
