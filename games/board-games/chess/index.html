Objective

Piece Movement

Pawns move forward and capture diagonally. Knights move in an L shape. Bishops move diagonally, rooks move horizontally or vertically, queens combine both, and kings move one square in any direction.

Special Moves

Chess also includes castling, en passant, and pawn promotion.
